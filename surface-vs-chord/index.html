Why World Athletics Requires Surface Measurement

Official road race courses are measured along the Shortest Possible Route (SPR) following exact road surface contours using a calibrated bicycle with a Jones Counter or steel tape. Optical Total Stations and 2D satellite coordinates ignore vertical profile curves and thus undercount actual distance traveled by runners. Laying out a race course via 2D horizontal projection makes the physical road course longer than intended.
